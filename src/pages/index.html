01About Inskavex

Technology that starts with the problem in front of you.

Inskavex is a software and technology company. We help businesses and organizations turn ideas, processes and plans into dependable digital products — from a first website to the systems a team relies on every day.

Our work begins with understanding: who will use what we build, what they need it to do, and what has to be true for it to keep working long after launch. From there, we design and engineer software that is clear to use, sound underneath and straightforward to look after.

We favor clarity over complexity, proven technology over passing trends, and steady, visible progress over big reveals.

What guides the work

Clarity
Interfaces people understand at a glance, and code other engineers can read.
Reliability
Software that behaves predictably and is tested before it reaches the people who depend on it.
Usability
Designed around real tasks, and accessible to as many people as possible.
Maintainability
Documented, sensibly structured and ready to grow as your needs change.

03Approach

How we work

A clear, collaborative process that keeps decisions visible and progress easy to follow. These are our working principles — every engagement is shaped around its own goals, scale and constraints.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start with the problem, the people it affects and the constraints that matter: goals, timelines, existing systems and budget.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree on scope, priorities and technical approach, and define together what a successful outcome looks like.

  3. 03

    Design

    User experience and system architecture are shaped side by side, so what looks right also works right underneath.

  4. 04

    Build iteratively

    Work progresses in small, reviewable increments. You see working software early and can steer it as it takes shape.

  5. 05

    Test & refine

    Functionality, accessibility, performance and security are checked throughout — not left to the final week.

  6. 06

    Deliver & improve

    We launch carefully, hand over clear documentation and plan what comes next — maintenance, improvements and new features.

06Questions

Good to know

Answers to common questions about starting a project. Anything else? Ask us directly.

What kinds of projects does Inskavex take on?

Websites, web and mobile applications, custom business software, automation and system integrations, and technical planning. If your project sits somewhere in between, get in touch — the best starting point is a description of the problem you want to solve.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Often, yes. Many projects involve extending, integrating with or gradually improving software that is already in place. We review what exists before recommending any changes.

How does a project usually begin?

With a conversation. We ask about your goals, users, timeline and constraints, then suggest a sensible next step — which might be a short discovery phase, a written proposal or simply some candid advice.

How are projects priced?

It depends on scope and complexity. Once we understand what you need, we outline the approach and provide a clear estimate before any work begins.

Is support available after launch?

Ongoing maintenance, improvements and support can be arranged once a project is delivered. We’ll discuss what level of support makes sense for your software and your team.

What should I include in my first message?

A short description of what you want to achieve, who it is for, any systems already involved and your ideal timeline. Rough notes are perfectly fine — we’ll take it from there.
